# Task 002 - Products, Reviews, Sales and CTA

## Goal

Create the main content of the long-form landing page.

The user should discover more information as they scroll.

## Page Flow

Introduction
↓
Products
↓
CTA
↓
Customer Reviews
↓
CTA
↓
Sales Numbers
↓
Why Choose Us
↓
CTA

## Products Section

Display featured bakery products.

Each product card contains:

- Product image
- Product name
- Short description
- Price
- CTA

Example:

- Croissant
- Chocolate Cake
- Sourdough Bread
- Strawberry Tart

## Product CTA

After the product section, create a strong CTA.

Example:

"Find Your Favorite"

Button:

"Explore All Products"

## Customer Reviews

Create a customer review section.

Each review should contain:

- Customer avatar
- Customer name
- Rating
- Short review

Example:

★★★★★

"Amazing taste and incredibly fresh.
I will definitely come back."

## Review CTA

After the reviews, display:

"Want to discover more?"

Button:

"See Product Details"

The CTA should visually connect the reviews
to the product information.

## Sales Numbers

Create a social-proof section displaying statistics.

Examples:

10,000+
Products Sold

2,500+
Happy Customers

5+
Years of Experience

50+
Products Available

Use large typography for the numbers.

## Why Choose Us

Display 3–4 reasons:

- Fresh Every Day
- Quality Ingredients
- Handmade With Care
- Customer Satisfaction

## CTA

Finish this task with another CTA.

Example:

"Ready to taste something special?"

Button:

"Get in Touch"

## Lazy Loading

All images below the initial viewport must use:

loading="lazy"

and:

decoding="async"

Do not lazy-load the hero image.

## Responsive

Products:

Desktop → multi-column grid
Tablet → flexible grid
Mobile → single column

Reviews:

Desktop → multiple cards
Mobile → stacked cards

## Acceptance Criteria

- Products are visible while scrolling
- CTA appears after products
- Reviews appear after products
- CTA appears after reviews
- Sales numbers appear after reviews
- Why Choose Us appears after sales numbers
- Final CTA appears
- Images below the fold use lazy loading
- Responsive on mobile
- No horizontal scrolling
- No console errors
