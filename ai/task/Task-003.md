# Task 003 - Contact, About Me, Farewell and Footer

## Goal

Complete the landing page.

The user should reach the final contact and farewell
after scrolling through the complete page.

## Page Flow

Final CTA
↓
Contact
↓
About Me
↓
Farewell
↓
Footer

## Contact Section

Display:

- Email
- Contact CTA
- Optional social links if provided

Do not invent real personal information.

Use:

your-email@example.com

as a placeholder if the real email is not provided.

## About Me

Display a short professional introduction.

Use:

"I am an Information Technology student with a strong interest
in full-stack web development.

I enjoy building modern web applications and working with
technologies such as React, Node.js, Express, and PostgreSQL.

I focus on creating clean, practical, and user-friendly
digital experiences while continuously improving my
software development skills."

Keep the content concise.

## Farewell Section

Create a visually memorable final section.

Example:

"Thank You for Visiting"

"Every visit, every order, and every kind word means a lot to us."

Then:

"Hope to see you again soon."

## Final CTA

Include:

"Let's Stay in Touch"

Button:

"Contact Us"

## Footer

Include:

- Logo
- Bakery name
- Email
- Navigation
- Copyright

## Final Page Experience

The complete page should feel like a continuous story:

Hero
↓
Discover
↓
Products
↓
CTA
↓
Trust
↓
Reviews
↓
More Trust
↓
Sales Numbers
↓
Why Us
↓
CTA
↓
Contact
↓
About Me
↓
Farewell

The user should never feel that the page suddenly ends.

## Performance

Use lazy loading for non-critical images.

Do not lazy-load important above-the-fold content.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Contact section works
- Email is displayed
- About Me is displayed
- Farewell is displayed
- Footer exists
- Final CTA exists
- Page works on mobile
- No horizontal scrolling
- No broken links
- No console errors
