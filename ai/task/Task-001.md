# Task 001 - Foundation, Navbar and Hero

## Goal

Build the beginning of the long-form landing page.

## Sections

1. Navbar
2. Hero / Header
3. Introduction

## Navbar

Include:

- Logo
- Home
- Products
- Reviews
- About
- Contact
- Primary CTA

Navigation must smoothly scroll to each section.

## Hero

Include:

- Large headline
- Supporting description
- Hero image
- Primary CTA
- Secondary CTA

Example:

"Freshly Baked, Made With Love."

"Discover handcrafted breads, pastries, and cakes made fresh every day."

Primary CTA:

"Explore Our Products"

Secondary CTA:

"Contact Us"

## Introduction

Briefly explain:

- Who we are
- What we offer
- What makes the bakery special

## Design

The hero should be visually strong and occupy most of
the first viewport.

The user should immediately understand:

1. What this business is
2. What it offers
3. What action they can take

## Performance

Do not lazy-load the hero image.

Use optimized image formats when available.

## Responsive

Support:

- Desktop
- Tablet
- Mobile

## Acceptance Criteria

- Navbar works
- Logo works
- Smooth scrolling works
- Hero works
- CTA works
- Mobile layout works
- No horizontal overflow
- No console errors
